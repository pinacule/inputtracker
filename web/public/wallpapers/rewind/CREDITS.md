# Rewind wallpaper — blue VCR menu on a CRT

The final skin ships one responsive pair, downloaded from the original on 2026-10-26.
Only the matching viewport variant is requested; Save-Data uses the canvas/texture fallback.

- Source: [Stack of vintage televisions in dark room](https://unsplash.com/photos/stack-of-vintage-televisions-in-dark-room-EM6GGiK1_go).
- Author: **Liana S (@cherstve_pechivo)**.
- Licence: [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), permitting modification and redistribution in the app.
- Original file: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1763132646264-4123ae7cf4a6 .
- Changes: small device badges retouched with adjacent plain chassis texture; grayscale, level adjustment and VCR-blue tint; brighter television highlights; landscape/portrait crops, resizing and metadata stripping. The screens show reflections/test marks, no entertainment imagery. CSS supplies a light blue tint, fine static scanlines and a slight outer vignette. All text-bearing surfaces remain opaque.
- The old blank-tape and warm-room exports were removed after the manager selected the blue VCR menu with CRT details. Original/reference files remain outside the app in ignored `.task/`.
